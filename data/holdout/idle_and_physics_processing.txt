Idle and Physics Processing

Godot offers two per-frame callbacks for game logic. _process(delta) runs once per rendered frame, so how often it runs depends on the frame rate. _physics_process(delta) runs at a fixed rate, 60 times per second by default, which makes it the right place for movement and collision code.

Choosing between them

Use _process for things that only need to look smooth, such as animating a user interface or updating a label. Use _physics_process for anything that interacts with the physics engine, such as moving a CharacterBody2D with move_and_slide, so that results stay consistent on fast and slow machines.

Changing the physics rate

The fixed rate is set in Project Settings under Physics, Common, Physics Ticks per Second. Raising it gives more precise collisions at the cost of more CPU time.
